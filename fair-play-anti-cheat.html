Competitive Integrity

Fair Play & Anti-Cheat System

Zero Hack Tolerance Live Spectator Review Device ID Permanent Ban

At BOOYAH SPORTS, fair competition is our top priority. Every player deserves a clean, skill-based battlefield where victory is determined strictly by aim, strategy, and game sense.

STRICT WARNING TO HACKERS: Using any third-party script, modded APK, auto-aim, wallhack, headshot file, or emulator bypass will result in an immediate hardware ID (HWID) permanent ban, zero prize payout, and forfeiture of all wallet funds.

1. Strictly Prohibited Offenses

  • Third-Party Mods & Hacks: Config files, GFX tools modifying game textures, auto-headshot scripts, speedhacks, or antenna hacks.
  • Emulator Bypass: Joining Mobile-Only custom rooms using PC Emulators (BlueStacks, LDPlayer, Nox) without joining designated PC lobbies.
  • Teaming & Collusion: Coordinating with enemy players or squads during Solo or Clash Squad matches to farm kills or secure points.
  • Account Sharing / Mismatched UIDs: Allowing a substitute player to play under your registered slot without prior admin authorization.

2. Spectator & Automated Audit System

Our multi-layered verification protocol ensures no cheater goes undetected:

  • Live Referee Spectators: Every tournament match is monitored live by official BOOYAH SPORTS referees who record gameplay footage.
  • Kill-Log Spectral Analysis: Match results undergo automated statistical analysis to flag unrealistic kill frequencies or headshot percentages.
  • Mandatory Screen Recording: Players in top prize spots or high-tier weekly finals may be requested to submit full unedited screen recordings with device touch points enabled.

3. Reporting a Hacker

If you encounter a suspicious player during a match:

  1. Record brief spectator gameplay or take a screenshot of the kill feed showing the offender's UID.
  2. Open the BOOYAH SPORTS App > Match History > Click "Report Match Discrepancy".
  3. Our security team will review the claim within 30 minutes. Verified reports award bonus wallet credits to the reporting player!
