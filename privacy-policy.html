Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025 Version 1.2 256-Bit Encrypted

Welcome to BOOYAH SPORTS ("Company", "We", "Our", or "Us"). We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal data when you download our Android APK, visit our official website (booyahsports.in), or participate in Free Fire MAX esports custom tournaments.

Key Privacy Guarantee: BOOYAH SPORTS strictly complies with the Information Technology Act, 2000, IT Intermediary Guidelines Rules 2021, and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 of India. We never sell, rent, or commercialize user data to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a seamless, secure, and automated esports experience, we collect necessary user details:

  • Account Registration Data: Mobile Phone Number (for OTP verification), User Name, Email Address, and Free Fire MAX In-Game UID and IGN (In-Game Name).
  • Financial & Transaction Data: UPI ID (VPA), Paytm/PhonePe phone numbers required solely for automated prize money withdrawals. We do NOT store your bank passwords, CVVs, or debit/credit card numbers.
  • Technical & Device Data: Android OS Version, Device Model, IP Address, Unique Device Identifiers (Advertising ID/IMEI hash) collected to enforce anti-cheat protection and prevent multi-accounting.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate esports gaming and app operational purposes:

  • Automated allocation and delivery of Free Fire MAX Custom Room ID and Passwords.
  • Automated 24x7 verification of match scores, kill counts, and instant UPI cashout processing.
  • Detecting, preventing, and banning hackers, modded APK users, or fraudulent duplicate accounts.
  • Sending crucial match alerts, SMS notifications, and customer support ticket resolutions.

3. Security Architecture & Encryption

We employ enterprise-grade security protocols to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration of your personal data:

  • 256-Bit SSL/TLS Encryption: All data transmitted between the BOOYAH SPORTS Android App and cloud server clusters is fully encrypted using TLS 1.3 protocol.
  • NPCI-Compliant Payment Gateways: Cash withdrawals are processed directly through RBI-regulated payment processors (Razorpay/Cashfree/UPI).
  • Secure Cloud Infrastructure: Database servers are protected behind firewall-secured private VPCs with strict access logging.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

BOOYAH SPORTS does not sell your personal data. Limited data sharing occurs exclusively in the following circumstances:

  • Payment Service Providers: Encrypted UPI IDs sent to licensed payout partners to facilitate instant prize money transfers.
  • Legal Compliance: If mandated by law enforcement authorities, court orders, or statutory regulatory bodies under applicable laws of India.

5. User Data Rights & Account Deletion

As a player on BOOYAH SPORTS, you retain full ownership and control over your personal data:

  • Right to Review & Update: You can edit your profile details, UPI ID, and email directly inside the App settings tab.
  • Right to Erasure (Account Deletion): You may request complete deletion of your account and personal history by emailing support@booyahsports.in or submitting a ticket in the Support Center. All personal data will be purged within 7 business days.
